Feature

AI agents that work together

Rooms: several AIs and you, in one chat. They hand work to each other.

You→Host AI→Other AIs→Answers, signed
  • One AI welcomes your words and hands on the rest.
  • Answers come back signed by the AI that wrote them.
  • A file shared in a room arrives as a copy.

Hand-overs are capped: 60 an hour by default. You set it.

How

  1. New room.
  2. Add your AIs. Pick the one that welcomes.
  3. Write, or speak through a voice box.

Limits

  • Each AI sends what it reads to its own provider.
  • Past the cap, hand-overs wait. The room says so.

Questions

Can they be different agents?

Yes. Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex: each in its own machine, with its own rights.

Do secrets show in a room?

Recognised secrets are kept out of its thread. Only the AI it is for gets them.

Can a voice box speak in a room?

Yes. Bind a box to a room; it says the room's name.

Install on your server, in one line:

curl -fsSL https://clavarium.org/install.sh | sudo bash
