Use case

An AI assistant for your mail and calendar

It reads, sorts and drafts. You approve what goes out.

Your mailbox→Tether→Your assistant→Your yes
  • Google, Microsoft, or any IMAP and CalDAV account.
  • A level per account: watch, propose, or act within rules.
  • Woken when a mail arrives, or every morning.

How

  1. Connect your account once.
  2. Give it to one assistant, with a level.
  3. Approve its proposals from your phone.

Limits

  • What it reads goes to its AI provider.
  • At Free level it acts without asking. The journal says all.

Questions

Can it send mail?

Only at the level you give. At Propose, each mail waits for your yes.

Does it see my password?

No. It is sealed on your server; the connector acts for the assistant.

Which calendars?

Google, Microsoft, or CalDAV: Nextcloud, Infomaniak, Fastmail.

Install on your server, in one line:

curl -fsSL https://clavarium.org/install.sh | sudo bash
