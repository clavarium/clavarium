Use case

Work, home, club: kept apart

One assistant per life. Each with its own machine, mail, calendar, files and keys.

  • Each assistant in its own machine. They never reach each other.
  • Each its own mail account, calendar and keys, given one by one.
  • Share a folder between two lives only if you choose.

How

  1. One conversation per life: work, home, club.
  2. Give each its own account and folders.
  3. Switch between them from your phone.

A club is a team too

  • Operators see everything. Give members a QR code instead.
  • Share one conversation with a QR code. Revoke at any time.
  • One screen shows who has what.

Limits

  • Each assistant sends what it reads to its own AI provider.
  • A folder you give to two lives, both can read.

Questions

Can the work assistant see my personal mail?

No. An account is given to no one until you give it, conversation by conversation.

Does a club member need an account?

No. A one-use QR code; their device sees that one conversation.

Can I copy a setup?

Yes, as one file: settings only, never a secret or a password.

Install on your server, in one line:

curl -fsSL https://clavarium.org/install.sh | sudo bash
