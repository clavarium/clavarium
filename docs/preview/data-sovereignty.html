Use case

AI agents without a US cloud

Your server, in your country. AI models hosted in Europe.

Your files→Your server→Agent→AI hosted in Europe
  • Runs on your own machine, at home or at a host you choose.
  • OpenCode or Mistral Vibe, on OVH AI Endpoints or Mistral.
  • Mail and calendars at OVH, Infomaniak or Nextcloud.

How

  1. Install on your machine.
  2. Add a key from a European provider.
  3. Start OpenCode on it.

Limits

  • What an agent reads goes to the provider you chose.
  • Models on your own machine: not yet, see the Roadmap.

Questions

Which providers in Europe?

OVH AI Endpoints and Mistral, both in France: for agents and for dictation.

Can I use a cloud server?

Yes, at any host, if the machine can run VMs (bare metal, or nested virtualisation).

Do Claude Code and Codex stay in Europe?

No. They use their makers' services. Pick OpenCode or Mistral Vibe for Europe.
