Use case

A private voice assistant for your home

A box in the kitchen or the living room. Your own AI answers.

Image to come: a voice box in a kitchen
You ask→Voice box→Your server→Calendar, reminders→Spoken answer
  • "What is on tomorrow?" It reads your calendar aloud.
  • "Remind me at six." The box tells you.
  • Questions, the radio, a podcast, a story.

It keeps no sound. Its lights dim at night.

How

  1. Install Clavarium on a small computer at home.
  2. Connect a box in each room you like.
  3. Give its assistant your calendar.

Limits

  • What you say goes to your dictation and AI providers.
  • Its wake word is French today: "Mésange". The button works too.

Questions

Which calendars?

Google, Microsoft, or any CalDAV calendar (Nextcloud, Infomaniak...).

Can it reach my mail too?

If you give it. Read only, or with your approval for each action.

Several rooms?

Yes, one box per room, each paired with your server.
