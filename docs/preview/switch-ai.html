Use case

Switch AI, keep your work

Move a conversation to another AI provider, and back, without setting everything up again.

  • Same conversation: pick another AI in its settings and confirm.
  • Your accounts, folders, files and thread stay where they are.
  • The new AI reads the thread and picks up from there.
  • Go back when you want: the previous AI's session is still there.

Limits

  • The old AI's private memory does not move.
  • The new AI needs its own account or API key.
  • What the new AI reads goes to its provider.
