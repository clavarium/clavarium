Use case

An AI assistant for elderly parents

Marcel, 82, talks to a small box in his living room. It answers.

Image to come: the voice box on Marcel's shelf
Marcel speaks→ Voice box→ Your server→ His assistant→ Spoken answer
  • Say its word or press the button. Then just talk.
  • It answers in short spoken sentences.
  • Radio, a podcast or a story, on request.

It keeps no sound. Its lights say when it listens.

How

  1. Install Clavarium once. Someone who knows Linux helps.
  2. Connect the box to your server.
  3. Marcel talks.

Limits

  • What he says goes to the AI provider you chose.
  • Needs Wi-Fi, and your server switched on.

Questions

Does it listen all the time?

It waits for its word or a press. It keeps no sound.

Does Marcel need a phone?

No. He only talks. The family sets it up.

What is the box?

A small audio board (ESP32-S3) with open-source firmware, MIT.
