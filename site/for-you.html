Say what you want. Your assistant prepares it.

An assistant that works on a small computer of your own. You talk to it from your phone or tablet.

  • Phone, tablet or computer
  • Speak or type
  • Reads mail, never sends
  • Your phone tells you
  • You send things yourself
  • Free

How you use it

  1. 01Say it aloud, or type it

    Open the app on your tablet and talk to your assistant on its text screen.

    Read this week's customer mail.
    Draft my replies.
    speak, or typeDictate

  2. 02Your phone tells you when it asks

    Go and do something else. When the assistant stops with a question, your phone tells you. Touch the message and you are in front of it.

    Your assistant has a questionMrs Martin asks for a discount. Offer one?
    It waits. It does not guess.

  3. 03Read what is ready, send it yourself

    The replies wait as files in the app, with a short report. You read them, then send them from your own mail.

    Report
    6 mails read · 2 replies drafted
    Reply to Mrs Martin
    Reply to Mr Lee
    You send.
What people hand to it →

It sees only what you give it

  • The folders and the mailbox you choose, and nothing else.
  • It reads your mail. It cannot send any.
  • What it reads is read by the AI service it uses.

It can work while you are away

  • It can start when a mail or a file arrives, or at a set hour.
  • Several AI models can answer its questions, only when they all agree.
  • If they disagree, it waits for you. Every question and answer is kept.

How you get it

clavarium is free. Someone sets it up once on a small computer of yours; then you open the app.
