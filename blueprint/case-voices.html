From voices to a story, and nobody rewrites what was said

Somebody records memories. Each recording becomes text, then a list of things that were said, then a story. Four workers do it, each in a machine of its own. None of them can change what came before it.

1. What happens, in order

For the person there are two gestures: drop a recording, and talk with the last worker. Everything between is woken by what arrives.

2. Who sees what

Each worker reads what came before it and writes one folder, its own. The storyteller cannot touch an item. The extractor cannot touch a transcript. That is what keeps a fact a fact.

3. What one item looks like

It carries its own identifier, who and when it is about, and the exact words it comes from. The checker looks for those words in the transcript. Not found: the item is not kept, and the person is told.

4. Why one agent alone cannot do this

  • It can write everywhere it can read. Asked for a better story, it may tidy an item, or the transcript. Here the storyteller has no right to write them, and that is enforced outside its machine.
  • It checks its own work. Here the check is a script: either the words are in the transcript, or they are not.
  • One maker, one model. Here the transcription, the extraction and the story can each use another service, and one can be replaced without touching the others.
  • It needs you at each step. Here each worker is woken by what arrives in its folder.
